            similarity search and ML pipeline.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            Addition places B's tail at A's tip (the parallelogram rule).
            Subtraction points from B to A. Scalar multiplication stretches
            or flips the arrow without changing its line.
          

        

        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            In Machine Learning, gradient descent literally does
            weights = weights − learning_rate × gradient.
            That's vector subtraction plus a scalar multiply, repeated a million times.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            A · B = |A||B|cos(θ). When the vectors point the same way the dot
            product is large and positive. At 90° it is exactly 0. When they point
            opposite ways it goes negative. Divide by both magnitudes and you get
            cosine similarity — a number between −1 and 1 that
            ignores length entirely.
          

        

        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            Cosine similarity powers semantic search, recommendation systems,
            RAG pipelines and vector databases. Every time you search
            "similar documents", something is computing dot products at scale.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            The cross product takes two 3D vectors and returns a third one that is
            perpendicular to both. Its length equals the area of the
            parallelogram they span. If the vectors are parallel, the result is
            the zero vector.
          

        

        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            Cross products compute surface normals in 3D graphics and ray tracing,
            determine winding order in mesh rendering, and solve torque and
            angular momentum problems in physics engines.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            A matrix is a machine that moves space. Multiply it by a vector and
            the vector lands somewhere new. The columns of the matrix tell you where
            the basis vectors î = (1,0) and
            ĵ = (0,1) end up. Every other vector follows along.
          

        

        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            Every layer of a neural network is a matrix multiply plus a bias.
            Every 3D object on screen is placed by a matrix. Every image filter is a
            small matrix called a kernel.

              scales area. A det of 5 means every shape gets 5× bigger.
              A det of 0 means the whole plane was squashed onto a single line —

            optimization and regression. In Graphics, determinants decide whether a
            triangle is front-facing or back-facing. In ML, near-zero determinants
            signal unstable, ill-conditioned models.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            Rotation keeps the length unchanged and only changes the direction.
            It's a matrix multiply:

            augment training data (rotated images), and implement positional encoding
            in modern transformer architectures.

EIGENVECTOR

            

              A direction that stays on the same line after transformation.
              It may get longer, shorter, or flip — but it never leaves its own line.
            

          

          

            
EIGENVALUE

            

              How much that direction stretches or shrinks.
              λ = 3 means it triples. λ = 0.5 means it halves.
              λ = −1 means it flips to the opposite side.
            

          

        


        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            PCA (Principal Component Analysis) finds the eigenvectors of a
            covariance matrix to reduce dimensionality. PageRank is an eigenvector
            of the web graph. Spectral clustering, stability analysis of neural nets,
            and vibration modes in physics engines all live here.

WHAT'S HAPPENING?

          

            Each vector is compared with the representative direction of every existing
            group using cosine similarity. If |cos| ≥ 0.85
            the vector belongs to that group — either pointing the same way
            (similar) or the exact opposite way (opposite). Otherwise a brand new
            group is created.
          

        

        

          
WHY IT MATTERS IN CS

          

            This is the seed of clustering and vector search. Embedding
            models turn words, images and users into vectors; grouping by direction is
            how recommendation engines decide what belongs together.
